How it was tested — before any row was ranked

The value curve is a ridge regression of log market cap on log TVL, breadth, age and seven-day growth, with one coefficient per large category, fitted on the 93 traded protocols in this snapshot (one per ticker, bridges / liquid staking / CEX left out — they price like custody, not like a launch). Method, in one paragraph

Each row's implied value is exp(intercept + 0.33·log TVL + …) from the fitted curve; its range is the leave-one-out residual quartiles applied to that point estimate. Launch odds come from a logistic fit on age and size over all 758 protocols. A farm plan's expected gross is odds × 15% × implied × deposit ÷ TVL; net subtracts gas (six transactions at the chain's estimated cost) and $4.10 of opportunity cost. FARM means net ≥ $10 on the plan's deposit, MARGINAL means net ≥ $0, anything else is SKIP. Gas costs and the 15% farmer share are stated estimates, not market data — change them in the sandbox and watch the verdict move.
